# FUT Gallery notes: card versions, reference prices and route costs

A useful price comparison needs more than a player name and a coin total. The identity of the card, the market, the age of the price and the meaning of the total all affect what a result tells you.

## Card identity and price are separate facts

A card directory identifies a specific version. A record in a directory does not, by itself, show that the version has a current market quote.

Likewise, a card missing from a suggested lineup is not evidence that it is unavailable. A lineup contains selected cards; it is not necessarily a complete catalogue.

## A suggested lineup can contain an older price

A price stored with a suggested lineup is an input to that calculation. It should not automatically be described as a current market quote. To compare two different amounts, first check the exact card version, market, source and observation time.

The time a page was downloaded is also different from the time its prices were observed. When the observation time is unknown, the age of the price remains unknown.

## Keep PS and PC observations separate

A quote from one market does not establish the price in another market. A missing price means there is insufficient evidence for that comparison; it does not mean the card is free.

## Route totals answer different questions

- **Purchase total:** the sum of the purchases included in a route.
- **Starting balance:** the coins needed to follow a particular order of purchases and sales.
- **Estimated net cost:** purchases minus expected sale proceeds, after the fees used in the calculation.

These values can differ. Expected sale proceeds are estimates, not coins already available to spend. Any route cost also depends on which cards are already recorded and which cards the player intends to keep.

## A low-cost result has a scope

A route found among a limited set of candidates describes that search. It does not prove that no cheaper route exists elsewhere. A meaningful comparison identifies its covered card versions, market, price snapshot and search limits.

This note explains terminology. It contains no current card quotes or purchase recommendations.
